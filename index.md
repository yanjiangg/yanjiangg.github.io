Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Stanford Center on Early Childhood. My research bridges developmental science, early childhood education, and data science to examine how social, cultural, and institutional conditions shape children’s development across family and education settings. My work focuses on how unequal resources and structural conditions shape learning opportunities and outcomes, whose knowledge and practices are privileged or marginalized, and how computational and AI-based research methods can inform more equitable policies and practices. I received my Ph.D. in Education Studies, with a specialization in Computational Social Science, from the University of California, San Diego.
