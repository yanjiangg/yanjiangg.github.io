# Teaching

Teaching is a process through which students learn to question assumptions, engage evidence critically, and develop their own ways of thinking about complex problems. My goal as an educator is to prepare thoughtful scholars, practitioners, and future leaders who can evaluate evidence, engage perspectives different from their own, and use knowledge to make informed decisions in research, policy, and practice. My teaching centers on three core pillars: (1) bridging theory and practice through project-based and community-engaged learning; (2) cultivating critical reflexivity about educational knowledge and evidence; and (3) creating inclusive learning environments that support perspective-taking, independent reasoning, and meaningful engagement across differences.

## Courses Taught

**Early Learning Environments for Diverse Learners**  
Explores child development theories and their applications to designing inclusive learning environments that support diverse learners.

**Cognitive Development and Education**  
Examines cognitive development from preschool through adolescence, including cultural variation and implications for educational practice and equity.

**Early Childhood Policy: Children, Contexts, and Policies in Diverse Societies**  
Examines how social and policy contexts shape children's developmental experiences, opportunities, and life trajectories across diverse societies.

**Advanced Quantitative Data Analysis**  
Graduate-level course covering advanced quantitative data analysis methods.

**Introduction to Research Design and Quantitative Data Analysis**  
Graduate-level course introducing foundational principles of research design and quantitative data analysis.
